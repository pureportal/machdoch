Machdoch modified the SDK on 2026-11-03:
- removed the TensorDict dependency; the denoiser and sampler pass plain tensor dictionaries;
- replaced the GPL-licensed plyfile writer with a NumPy PLY writer and added Gaussian cleanup;
- loads every model from a local package instead of the Hugging Face Hub, with no gated-access checks;
- loads the DINOv3 ViT-H+/16 encoder from the timm checkpoint, renamed to the transformers layout;
- uses the Apache-2.0 FLUX.2 klein 4B autoencoder instead of downloading it from FLUX.2-dev;
- reports progress per sampling step, builds models on the target device and uploads weights from pinned memory;
- removed the torch.compile sampling path, the command-line sampler and the demo.

The Idol, DINOv3 and FLUX.2 autoencoder weights are downloaded separately and keep their own licences:
Idol (Apache-2.0), DINOv3 (DINOv3 License, Meta Platforms) and FLUX.2 klein 4B (Apache-2.0).
