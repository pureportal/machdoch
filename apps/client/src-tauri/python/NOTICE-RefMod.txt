Machdoch implements independent reference storage, selection, compression,
conditioning, and editor controls. Research covered the v5 bundle schema,
latent refinement, numbered prompt labels, strengths, and token accounting.
Upstream source licences and attribution are retained without relicensing.

The existing Fizgig MiniMax model implementation was modified on 2026-10-06
to support packed audio reference projection, positions, modality tags and
clean conditioning timesteps alongside visual references. Its existing licence
and third-party provenance notices still apply. ComfyUI-derived MiniMax code
has unresolved GPL provenance; retaining notices does not resolve that issue.

On 2026-10-06, Machdoch modified the MiniMax sampler to stop sampling when
progress or cancellation callbacks fail, and to propagate cancellation
exceptions to the caller. The sampler's existing licence and provenance
obligations remain unchanged.

On 2026-10-06, Machdoch replaced the ComfyUI-derived audio VAE implementation
with an adapter to Diffusers' AutoencoderKLMiniMaxH3Audio. The architecture is
provided by the Apache-2.0 dependency rather than copied from the former port:
https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/main/src/diffusers/models/autoencoders/autoencoder_kl_minimax_h3_audio.py
The adapter uses the current folded fp32 checkpoint weights, preserves stereo
normalization and loads only the required encoder or decoder components.
On 2026-10-06, Machdoch replaced the ComfyUI-derived video VAE implementation
with an adapter to Diffusers' AutoencoderKLMiniMaxH3:
https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/main/src/diffusers/models/autoencoders/autoencoder_kl_minimax_h3.py
Checkpoint layout research used Diffusers' current conversion script:
https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/main/scripts/convert_minimax_h3_to_diffusers.py
The architecture, spatial tiling and temporal blending come from the
Apache-2.0 dependency. Machdoch's loader converts the original projection
layout, validates normalization and weights, and loads the selected component.
The adapter preserves H3's still and short-clip geometry and bounds frame
previews to two temporal chunks. It retains float32 weights with float16
autocast at GPU call sites, increasing decoder residency over the former port.

The remaining MiniMax model and sampling ports retain unresolved GPL
provenance and combined-work obligations. Replacing the VAEs does not
establish clearance for distributing the whole runtime.

MiniMax-H3 model weights have separate terms, including geographical and
commercial restrictions. This integration does not grant rights to obtain,
