# Local KREA 2 LoRA training

## Research

- KREA 2 RAW is the trainable base. KREA recommends training a LoRA on RAW and applying it to the distilled Turbo model for inference. The open model uses a 12B transformer, Qwen3-VL text encoder, and Qwen-Image VAE. Its weights are large and its license has separate community and enterprise terms. [KREA open weights](https://www.krea.ai/krea-2-open-source), [KREA model repository](https://github.com/krea-ai/krea-2)
- Diffusers' [KREA 2 DreamBooth LoRA guide](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/main/examples/dreambooth/README_krea2.md) documents a local RAW LoRA recipe: 1024 pixels, rank/alpha 32, 1,000 steps, learning rate 3e-4, gradient checkpointing, cached latents, and Turbo validation. It supports per-image captions, attention-only targets, checkpoints, offload, and NF4 through bitsandbytes. Its guide says lower resolution saves memory; different concept sets need experimentation.
- [Musubi Tuner](https://github.com/kohya-ss/musubi-tuner/blob/main/docs/krea2.md) has a dedicated KREA 2 training script, RAW single-file loading, FP8/ConvRot int8, block swap, and Turbo preview. It remains experimental. [Ostris AI Toolkit](https://github.com/ostris/ai-toolkit) also supports KREA 2 local LoRA training. These are useful for users whose models are in ComfyUI single-file form or who need different memory controls.
- The official [ComfyUI KREA 2 workflow](https://docs.comfy.org/tutorials/image/krea/krea-2) uses the KREA 2 transformer, Qwen3-VL encoder, Qwen-Image VAE, and a model-only LoRA loader. [LoraLoaderModelOnly](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/ComfyUI/blob/master/nodes.py) applies a LoRA to the diffusion model with adjustable strength. The [KREA 2 template](https://github.com/Comfy-Org/workflow_templates/blob/main/templates/image_krea2_turbo_t2i_int8.json) demonstrates this inference path. Kijai's [ComfyUI-FluxTrainer](https://github.com/kijai/ComfyUI-FluxTrainer) focuses on Flux and SDXL rather than providing a KREA 2 trainer.

## Media Studio implementation

The Train section creates KREA 2 LoRAs for styles, faces, characters, and objects on the desktop host. It requires a **local Diffusers-format KREA 2 RAW folder** with `model_index.json` and all model components. Images and editable captions are copied to the Media Studio asset folder; the trigger phrase is added to each caption when absent. The local Python runtime runs a bundled copy of Diffusers' [training script at commit `4295ee3`](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/4295ee3ec58efa6577bc459e9b84ca3f63aa9a96/examples/dreambooth/train_dreambooth_lora_krea2.py) under its Apache 2.0 license, with local reporting disabled. The training process has Hugging Face and datasets offline modes, no telemetry or tracking, and no hub push. The user can select 4-bit NF4 weights, rank, resolution, learning rate, step count, and attention-only layers. It saves periodic checkpoints for interrupted jobs.

The result is inspected and imported as a KREA 2 LoRA into Assets, then can be selected in Basic with the installed KREA 2 inference model and adjusted by strength. Existing asset import also supports external KREA 2 LoRAs and compatible fine-tuned KREA 2 inference checkpoints. Dataset copies and training checkpoints are removed after successful import; failed or stopped jobs can be removed or resumed from a checkpoint.

## Limits and verification

- Training needs local RAW weights and a GPU/runtime that can load them. Existing Turbo inference weights cannot serve as the RAW training base. Media Studio does not download the RAW repository automatically or convert a ComfyUI single-file RAW checkpoint into Diffusers format.
- This flow trains the transformer LoRA. A “face” LoRA is a subject LoRA trained on face images; it is not a separate face embedding or identity model. The flow does not train the Qwen3-VL encoder, VAE, or a full KREA 2 checkpoint. Other architecture LoRAs and fine-tuned checkpoints remain importable through Assets, but the Train section currently creates KREA 2 LoRAs only.
- Low-memory NF4 depends on bitsandbytes support for the selected accelerator. CPU-only training is technically possible in the underlying script but impractical for this model. KREA 2 RAW training on this workspace was not possible because RAW weights are not installed. Actual LoRA generation could therefore not be verified here.
- The Train section is desktop-local. Remote Fleet sessions do not send training data or model weights to a host.
- Verification covered the live Train UI in Playwright at desktop and narrow widths, Tauri IPC validation and job cleanup, offline dataset loading, the local runner, KREA 2 LoRA key mapping, and an AMD GPU NF4 operation. The installed KREA 2 inference entry could not be used because its package path was missing; neither full training nor image generation was verified.
