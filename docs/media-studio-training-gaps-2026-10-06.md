# Media Studio training gaps — 2026-10-06

The coverage snapshot has **32 missing training identifiers out of 52 generation architectures**, with 20 reported as integrated and `scopeComplete: false`. All 32 identifiers are accounted for below. None is declared ineligible because its trainer is missing. Published training evidence varies: some have exact LoRA/full-weight recipes, some have distillation recipes, and others need a checkpoint-specific implementation. This audit does **not** establish training, embeddings, or pretrained quality for every open model.

## Evidence and local integration

The baseline is [coverage.json](validation/training-flux2/coverage.json), which records its audit time and source hashes. Every recorded architecture has `pretrainedQualityVerified: false`. [The October 4 research note](media-studio-training-research-2026-10-04.md) is historical context, not proof of current integration.

The inspected [training dispatcher](../apps/client/src-tauri/python/media_training.py) has no direct entry for any of these 32 exact identifiers. Its `flux-2` entry does not establish a route for `flux-2-dev`. Completion currently assumes one of three artifacts: `pytorch_lora_weights.safetensors`, `learned_embeds.safetensors`, or `model/model_index.json`. An AR PEFT adapter, native video checkpoint, or Transformers SVG model requires a different artifact contract.

The [catalog](../apps/client/src-tauri/python/open_media_models.json) binds 28 identifiers to repositories/revisions; seven have no catalog binding. Those seven are mapped to upstream candidates below, without claiming which custom runner/checkpoint is installed. [The model loader](../apps/client/src-tauri/python/media_open_models.py) requires a local Diffusers directory, matching `model_index.json` pipeline class, available Python class, safetensors, and no remote code. [Video generation](../apps/client/src-tauri/python/media_open_video.py) loads LoRAs only for CogVideoX and rejects addons for other families. [Rust catalog validation](../apps/client/src-tauri/src/media/open_models.rs) checks inference constraints, not training eligibility. Dataset processing, trainer dispatch, package conversion, and addon reload are implementation work.

Primary sources were inspected online on October 6. Diffusers and trainer `main` pages establish upstream routes, **not their presence or compatibility in the installed pin**. Exact model-card revision reads succeeded for 10 catalog rows: the three Qwen entries, three Wan entries, two CogVideoX 1.5 entries, Mochi, and Sulphur. Browser cache/access failures prevented equivalent revision-content checks for the other 18 rows; current publisher cards and catalog pins were used with that limitation. A later GitHub API audit retrieved the pinned CogVideoX T2V trainer and verified Git blob `3fd542173e348e3b4544e3ce171eb1f6b6cb430c`. The complete pinned example tree also contains its separate I2V trainer, but no Wan training example. This establishes source presence, not dependency or runtime parity.

Local verification used `D:\Models\machdoch\runtime\environment\Scripts\python.exe`, the above Diffusers pin, AMD RX9070 with **15.922 GiB physical VRAM**, and **31.31 GiB RAM**. Unauthenticated publisher requests returned 401 for 9B/SD3/FLUX1; that observation cannot be extended to all 32 models. The [training verification report](media-studio-flux2-training-verification-2026-10-06.md) records completed local jobs and their limits.

## Family contracts

### FLUX.2 dev

Use Diffusers `train_dreambooth_lora_flux2.py`; paired editing has `train_dreambooth_lora_flux2_img2img.py`. T2I uses image/caption or subject images; editing needs condition image, target image, and instruction. Dev uses **Mistral Small 3.1**, unlike klein's encoder. Preserve checkpoint latent normalization/packing, spatial IDs, prompt features, guidance conditioning, and fused projection module names; cache keys must include their configuration. Export transformer LoRA in the pipeline's native key layout with the exact base revision; validate editing separately from T2I. The reference FP8 path requires CUDA compute capability at least 8.9. Its remote encoder uses authenticated HF inference and does not verify a fully local route. No credible numeric minimum can be taken from the README's unfinished “XXGB” estimate. [Diffusers dev/klein training recipes](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/main/examples/dreambooth/README_flux2.md).

The catalog model is the 32B guidance-distilled dev model. Access is gated and its terms differ from klein; no dev weight request was made here. Resolve local encoder availability, AMD quantization/backward support, and memory before a real job. [Publisher model card](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-dev).

### GLM-Image

The publisher's route trains a **9B autoregressive prior LoRA**, freezing the 7B diffusion decoder. Image/caption data becomes vision/VQ image tokens, trained with cross entropy; substituting the generic flow objective is incorrect. Attention projections and `gate_up_proj/down_proj` are named targets. Export a PEFT adapter for `GlmImageForConditionalGeneration` from `vision_language_encoder`; the documented reload merges that adapter and replaces the pipeline's prior. A generic diffusion transformer LoRA filename alone is insufficient. Reference AR LoRA training reports about 35 GB VRAM at batch two and 60 GB at batch four. [Publisher finetuning instructions](https://github.com/zai-org/GLM-Image/blob/main/finetune/README.md).

Editing also needs source-image grids, image-prior tokens, and glyph conditioning. An image/caption AR recipe does not establish reference-conditioned training or joint decoder finetuning. Implement token/grid fixtures and export binding now; the documented reference allocation exceeds this GPU. [Diffusers GLM conditioning API](https://huggingface.co/docs/diffusers/main/en/api/pipelines/glm_image).

### HiDream

The exact route is I1 **Full**, using `train_dreambooth_lora_hidream.py` with image/caption or subject data. Cache all four text encoders' outputs: two CLIP encoders, T5, and Llama hidden states. Preserve attention masks, pooled/token features, FLUX-derived VAE normalization, and the Full model's flow objective. Export native HiDream transformer LoRA, with encoder/tokenizer revisions retained in metadata; Full, Dev, Fast, and O1 are not interchangeable. The NF4 reference peaks around 36.9 GB after backward, compared with 59.9 GB without quantization; that is not a 16 GB training demonstration. [Diffusers HiDream recipe](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/main/examples/dreambooth/README_hidream.md), [training implementation](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/main/examples/dreambooth/train_dreambooth_lora_hidream.py).

The diffusion weights are published separately from Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct, whose access and terms must be satisfied independently. Encoder access is a potential external prerequisite; conditioning and offload implementation remain possible. [Publisher Full card](https://huggingface.co/HiDream-ai/HiDream-I1-Full).

### Ideogram 4

Diffusers publishes `train_dreambooth_lora_ideogram4.py`: NF4 QLoRA on the **conditional** transformer, with the unconditional transformer frozen. Preserve Qwen3-VL selected-layer features and asymmetric guidance. Image captions should retain the structured JSON schema, including composition/elements/bounding boxes. The recipe requires `--disable_training_autocast`; bf16 autocast can corrupt outputs and fp16 can produce NaNs. Export native conditional-transformer LoRA. The example asset `ideogram-ai/ideogram-4-nf4-diffusers` differs from the catalog's `ideogram-ai/ideogram-4-nf4`; verify component layout and conversion before treating them as the same package. NF4, SDNQ, and torchao training are separate backend contracts, none proven on the local AMD runtime. [Diffusers Ideogram recipe](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/main/examples/dreambooth/README_ideogram4.md).

The publisher NF4 asset is gated. Access is an external prerequisite if weights are absent; package reconciliation, JSON caption validation, precision handling, and export reload are implementation work. [Publisher NF4 card](https://huggingface.co/ideogram-ai/ideogram-4-nf4).

### Qwen-Image

Original and 2512 use `QwenImagePipeline`; the original has a [Diffusers LoRA trainer](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/main/examples/dreambooth/train_dreambooth_lora_qwen_image.py). Reusing its architecture for 2512 is a route to verify, not evidence that this exact checkpoint has trained locally.

Musubi documents `qwen_image_train_network.py` and full-weight `qwen_image_train.py`. T2I requires image/caption, Qwen2.5-VL token features/masks, correct VAE normalization, and image-shape metadata. Edit-2511 needs ordered reference images, instruction, target image, and both vision-language and VAE reference caches; preserve the documented roughly one-megapixel reference resizing. Loss must supervise target latents, with references retained as conditions. Its full edit-2511 route is explicitly unverified upstream. Convert Musubi adapter keys/scales before Diffusers reload; full models need all frozen pipeline components. Documented 1024px fp8/block-swap examples range from 30/24 GB down to 12 GB with substantial swapping; they do not prove AMD performance or host-memory fit. Full-training state saving can need about 40 GB RAM, beyond this machine. [Musubi Qwen training guide](https://github.com/kohya-ss/musubi-tuner/blob/main/docs/qwen_image.md).

### Qwen-Image 2.1

The upstream recipe provides separate T2I and img2img LoRA scripts. Its visual generator is 7B, rather than the original 20B; the publisher supports up to ten references, requiring its own reference limit and pipeline binding. This variant needs an **RGBA, four-channel VAE**, dimensions divisible by 32, Qwen3-VL processor features with variable lengths/masks, and block-causal attention. Training uses unshifted sigmas despite shifted sampling. Editing encodes the condition into both VL image tokens and clean VAE latents, preceding noisy target latents; loss covers the target only. Use instruction/reference/target data and preserve alpha, rather than coercing everything to RGB. Export native 2.1 transformer LoRA with its processor/VAE and scheduler identity. Validation is documented at 40 steps with CFG disabled. Bind the missing local catalog entry before claiming compatibility; neither full training nor generic embeddings is established by this recipe. Large-model memory and AMD kernel behavior still need measurement. [Diffusers 2.1 training recipe](https://github.com/huggingface/diffusers/blob/main/examples/dreambooth/README_qwenimage21.md), [publisher checkpoint](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen-Image-2.1).

### SANA-Sprint

Sprint has an actual upstream student-training route: `train_scripts/train_scm_ladd.sh` with its Sprint configuration, plus Diffusers `train_sana_sprint_diffusers.py`. Image/caption WebDataset or Parquet inputs feed teacher/student consistency and adversarial training, not the ordinary Sana flow recipe. Retain Gemma-2-2B-it features, the DC-AE's 32 latent channels and 32× compression, continuous-time parameterization, teacher identity, and consistency sampler. The publisher converter selects `SanaSprint_1600M_P1_D20`, `scheduler_type=scm`, and a full Diffusers pipeline. Validate the local **one-step** contract explicitly; published two-step examples do not establish it after personalization. ## Actionable priorities and acceptance

1. **Bind every route before expanding support claims.** Record identifier, exact checkpoint/file/revision, trainer commit, trainable component/method, conditioning schema, sampler, export loader, access requirements and proof state.
