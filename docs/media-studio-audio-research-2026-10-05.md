# Media Studio audio research — 2026-10-05

AudioLDM 2 is the only audio-generation family with completed Machdoch inference evidence. MuseTalk consumes supplied vocals; its successful lip-sync run does not establish music or speech generation. The models below remain integration work, not catalog entries or tested Machdoch runtimes.

| Model | Publisher route | Training evidence | Machdoch gap |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| ACE-Step 1.5 base, SFT, Turbo, and XL | [Official runtime](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5) exposes music generation, lyrics, reference audio, and distinct model variants. Its hardware table includes AMD configurations and smaller models; larger XL configurations have different memory needs. | [Publisher LoRA tutorial](https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step-1.5/blob/main/docs/en/LoRA_Training_Tutorial.md) describes dataset preparation and adapter training. This audit does not establish a full-weight trainer. | Needs a pinned runtime and acquisition manifest, typed music and lyric inputs, addon import, training jobs, and actual song inference followed by lip sync. The smaller published configurations are not ruled out by this machine's 16 GB GPU alone. |
| Stable Audio Open 1.0 | [Publisher model card](https://huggingface.co/stabilityai/stable-audio-open-1.0) provides `StableAudioPipeline` and stereo output, separate from AudioLDM 2. | [Stable Audio Tools](https://github.com/Stability-AI/stable-audio-tools) provides training, pretrained checkpoint finetuning, and unwrapping for inference. An exact LoRA route was not established. | Needs package recognition, adapter and output-shape handling, training export validation, and real stereo generation. The publisher package requires Hugging Face access and model terms. |
| MiniMax Speech 2.8 HD / Turbo | [Official speech API](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/speech-t2a-http) uses speech model IDs, text, voice settings, and audio settings. | No local weights, full-weight trainer, or LoRA route established by this API audit. | A video-provider integration does not implement this audio endpoint. Needs a speech contract, provider adapter, credentials, and real requests. |
| MiniMax Music 3.0 | [Official music API](https://platform.minimax.io/docs/api-reference/music-generation) documents a separate music-generation request. | No local weights, full-weight trainer, or LoRA route established by this API audit. | Needs a music contract, provider adapter, credentials, and real requests. |

Publisher capabilities are research evidence. They do not establish working Machdoch support, measured generation speed, listening quality, legal entitlement to use weights, or accurate singing lip sync. No new audio model was downloaded, trained, or executed during this continuation.
