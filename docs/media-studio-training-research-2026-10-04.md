An absent script in this inventory is not evidence that a family cannot be trained. Distillation, quantization, pipeline components, target layers, dataset representation, and export formats require family-specific work. Musubi describes its trainers as experimental; copying a trainer name into the UI would not satisfy generation or training support.

For speech-driven video, [Wan's S2V route](https://github.com/Wan-Video/Wan2.2) and [LTX's conditioning pipelines](https://github.com/Lightricks/LTX-2/blob/main/packages/ltx-pipelines/docs/pipelines.md) are concrete upstream candidates. They are not implemented as Machdoch audio-input or lip-sync nodes. LoRA training also does not substitute for full-weight finetuning.
